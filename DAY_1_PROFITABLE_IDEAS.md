## 🏆 Model 1: The 60-Second ATS Resume & Job Tailor (*"ApplySwift"*)

### 1. The Routine Problem
* Millions of people apply for jobs every single day on LinkedIn, Indeed, and glassdoor.
* 90% of resumes get automatically rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) software because keywords don't match the job post.
* Job seekers spend 2–3 hours every night manually editing Word documents.

### 2. How the 100% Digital Tool Works
1. User pastes their current resume + the job post URL or description.
2. The AI matches keywords, rewrites bullet points to highlight relevant skills, and calculates a match score (e.g., *94% ATS Match*).
3. In 20 seconds, it outputs a clean, modern, ATS-proof PDF resume + tailored cover letter.

### 3. How You Make Money on Day 1
* Free preview: Shows the ATS score and 3 keyword improvements for free.
* To download the tailored PDF & Cover Letter:
  * **Option A:** **\$4.99 one-time download** (impulse buy).
  * **Option B:** **\$14.99 for a 1-Month Unlimited Pass**.
* **Zero effort from you:** The server and AI generate the PDF automatically. You just see money in your Stripe dashboard.
* **The Math:** 10 downloads/day $\times$ \$5 = **\$50/day (\$1,500/month)**.

## 🥉 Model 3: Subscription & Hidden Bank Fee Audit Tool (*"SubZero"*)

### 1. The Routine Problem
* The average person wastes **\$40 to \$120 every month** on forgotten streaming subscriptions, gym memberships, trial auto-renewals, and hidden recurring fees.
* People know they are losing money, but hate reading through 12 pages of confusing bank statements.

### 2. How the 100% Digital Tool Works
1. User pastes or securely uploads their recent bank/credit card CSV statement (or enters recurring bills).
2. The web app instantly detects recurring patterns, flags forgotten trials, and calculates annual wasted cash:  
   *"You are spending \$684/year on 4 services you rarely use."*
3. Generates **one-click certified cancellation emails & letters** with legal language that forces the companies to cancel and stop charging them.

### 3. How You Make Money on Day 1
* Free scan: Shows the first 2 detected subscriptions for free.
* Unlock Full Audit + Auto-Cancellation Letter Generator: **\$6.99 one-time** or **\$3.99/month**.
* **Direct ROI:** The customer spends \$7 to save \$600. Extremely easy to sell.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Compliant with DPDPA 2023 & IT Act 2000

At DepositShield India (operated at https://depositshield.onrender.com), your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard your personal information when using our automated document drafting platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Tenancy & Dispute Information: Landlord name, landlord phone number, rented property address, deposit amount paid, amount withheld, and disputed reasons.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To dynamically draft and format your 7-Day Pre-Litigation Legal Demand Notice, Consumer Court Petition, and Police Complaint.
  • To contact you via Phone or WhatsApp when you request a callback or assistance regarding your deposit dispute.
  • To transmit electronic delivery confirmations and transaction receipts.
  • To maintain platform security and prevent fraudulent misuse.

3. Payment Security & Financial Data

DepositShield India never collects, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, debit card PINs, netbanking passwords, or UPI MPINs. All payment transactions are securely handled through Razorpay, an RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator complying with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards and 256-bit bank-grade SSL encryption.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Data is shared only with:

  • Authorized payment processors (Razorpay) solely to process your payment transaction.
  • Law enforcement or judicial authorities when required by mandatory legal subpoenas under Indian law.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL/TLS protocols, firewall protection, and access controls to prevent unauthorized access or alteration of your records. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill document delivery and customer service obligations.

6. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data stored on our servers. To exercise these rights, please submit a data request through our Online Support & Grievance Portal.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Applicable to all Digital Products

DepositShield India (operated at https://depositshield.onrender.com) provides online automated legal self-help document drafting services. We do not manufacture or ship physical tangible goods through postal carriers.

1. Mode of Delivery

All legal documents and services offered on our platform are delivered electronically and digitally:

  • Instant On-Screen Unlock: Upon successful payment confirmation via Razorpay, the full, unblurred legal document is unlocked instantly on your browser screen.
  • PDF Download: You receive 1-click access to download your court-ready 2-Page Pre-Litigation Legal Demand Notice, Consumer Court Petition (Form 1), and Police Complaint (BNS 316) in print-ready PDF format.
  • WhatsApp & Email Dispatch: A digital copy along with pre-filled WhatsApp and Speed Post sending instructions is dispatched to your registered WhatsApp mobile number and email address within minutes.

2. Delivery Timeline

Delivery is instantaneous (0 to 5 minutes) upon payment authorization. There are zero shipping delays or transit times.

3. Delivery Charges

Electronic delivery of all digital notices, draft templates, and PDF downloads is 100% free of shipping charges. The price displayed during checkout is all-inclusive.

4. Non-Receipt of Documents

In rare situations where a network interruption prevents automatic browser unlocking or email delivery:

  • Please check your browser downloads and spam/promotions folder in your email.
  • Submit a ticket through our Online Support Helpdesk with your Razorpay Payment ID or Notice Ref Number.
  • Our technical team will re-dispatch your customized legal document directly to your confirmed account within 30 to 60 minutes.

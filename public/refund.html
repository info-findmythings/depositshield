Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Compliant with Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020

Thank you for choosing DepositShield India (accessible at https://depositshield.onrender.com). We are committed to transparency and customer satisfaction regarding our automated legal self-help document drafting services.

1. Cancellation Policy

DepositShield India provides instant electronic delivery of customized digital legal documents (Pre-Litigation Demand Notices, Consumer Court Petitions, and Police Complaint drafts). Since the document generation and unlocking process occurs immediately upon successful online payment confirmation:

  • Orders cannot be cancelled once the document has been unlocked, viewed, or downloaded.
  • If an order was initiated by mistake and payment was not completed, no cancellation fee applies.

2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We offer a hassle-free refund under the following valid circumstances:

  • Technical Generation Failure: If payment was successfully deducted from your account, but the website failed to unlock or generate your document due to a server error or technical glitch.
  • Duplicate Payment: If you were charged more than once for the same transaction or reference number due to network latency or gateway timeout, the duplicate transaction will be refunded in full.
  • Incorrect Amount Deducted: If the payment deducted exceeds the advertised plan price on our portal.
100% Refund Guarantee on Technical Errors:

If you experience any technical difficulty unlocking your document, simply reach out to our support team with your payment transaction ID. If we cannot deliver your document within 2 hours, we will issue a complete 100% refund.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds shall not be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Change of mind after the document has already been generated, unlocked, or copied.
  • Landlord refusal to comply with your legal notice (as third-party actions are beyond our control).
  • Incorrect party or tenancy details entered by the user during the drafting wizard (users can request support to correct typographical errors free of charge).

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us within 48 hours of the transaction with the following details:

  • Full Name of the user
  • Registered Mobile / WhatsApp Number
  • Razorpay Payment ID (e.g., pay_XXXXXXXXXX) or Order ID
  • Brief explanation of the technical issue faced

Send your refund request to support@depositshield.in or WhatsApp our support line at +91 98765 43210.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is received and verified, we will initiate the refund via Razorpay within 24 to 48 hours. The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank's clearance cycle.
